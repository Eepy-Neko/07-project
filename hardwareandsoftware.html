Hardware & Software

To make good digital art, you'll need reliable hardware and software. However, be sure to keep in mind that it doesn't need to be anything too fancy. There are thousands of different drawing programs out there, ranging from expensive professional programs to free apps that will sometimes come pre-installed on a device. The same goes for whatever hardware you choose. As long as you know how to work with your chosen device and software, you can make good digital art.

Software

Here are a few different drawing programs that come highly recommended:

Clip Stuido Paint
Clip Studio Paint is a well known drawing program that is used by millions of artists worldwide. They have their own list of resources to help you in your drawing process, such as tutorials and 3D models reference models, along with many different tools and brushes that you can customize to your liking.
Krita
Krita is a free and open-source program for digital painting and frame-by-frame animation. While it has a little bit of a learning curve, most artists who use it have found that it's powerful and worth learning.
Photoshop
Photoshop is an Adobe product, so while it does come with a subscription fee attached, it has a lot of freedom within it to make your art. It's also able to be used on a standalone tablet, like an iPad.
